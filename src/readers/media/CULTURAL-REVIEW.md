# Cultural review checklist

The archives are runtime-integrated but remain `source-backed-draft`. Before they are described as culturally reviewed, each pack must be checked by a knowledgeable person from, or specialist in, the represented tradition.

## All packs

- Confirm native names, diacritics, capitalisation and Spanish/English presentation.
- Confirm that every named species, deity, spirit, legendary being and physical element is actually supported by at least one cited source.
- Replace broad corpus-level citations with item-level citations wherever the corpus does not clearly establish an individual entry.
- Keep the historical or folklore element separate from Online Arcana's invented rank order, naipes correspondence and divination method.
- Remove sacred, lineage-restricted, initiatory or otherwise inappropriate material.
- Confirm that the two public states describe a real physical distinction in the fictional medium.
- Confirm that no ritual adds a click, choice, pause, reroll or second draw.
- Confirm that no text claims the complete fictional system is an authentic historical practice.

## Pack-specific cautions

- **Brennos:** distinguish Helvetii and Central European La Tène evidence from later Insular Celtic folklore. The current trees, flowers, birds and beasts are a comparative folklore ranking, not a recovered Helvetii divination system. Verify every species and every continental deity individually.
- **Yejide:** do not present the seed casting as Ifá. Confirm that every fish, reptile, bird and land creature occurs in an appropriate Yorùbá ecological, artistic or narrative source. Review every òrìṣà and sacred figure with a Yorùbá specialist.
- **Ngaru:** obtain Māori review for every customary pattern, atua and legendary figure. The paired outer-painted and inner-painted shells are Online Arcana fiction. Avoid tā moko, iwi-specific or restricted imagery unless explicitly approved.
- **Ame:** preserve the single cast for the whole spread. Confirm the botanical names Sakura, Hasu, Fuji and Ajisai. Keep kami, legendary figures, kitsune traditions and yōkai categories distinct rather than calling all of them Shinto deities.
- **Amaru:** use documented khipu construction without claiming that the four selected colours or knot counts had these spiritual meanings. Review Quechua spelling, Inka divine names and any living Andean sacred concepts.
- **Nahid:** the smoke-reading method and four-element rank system are fictional. Verify the historical period, transliteration and the distinction between yazatas, Amesha Spentas, abstract principles and other divine or cosmological figures. Respect living Zoroastrian ritual restrictions.
- **Mictli:** keep the file label exactly `Cempasúchil`. Verify Nahuatl spelling and the direct iconography used for every deity disk. The eagle, cempasúchil, jaguar and cacao count families are Online Arcana correspondences, not a claimed Mexica divination set.
