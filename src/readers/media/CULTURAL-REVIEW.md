# Cultural review checklist

Before runtime integration, review each pack with a knowledgeable person from or specialist in the represented tradition.

## All packs

- Confirm native names, diacritics and Spanish/English descriptions.
- Confirm that cited elements are genuinely documented.
- Separate documented context from the fictional tarot bridge.
- Remove or replace sacred, lineage-restricted or inappropriate material.
- Confirm that one medium item remains one item even when it carries a composition of motifs.
- Confirm that the ritual creates concealed chance without adding user interaction.
- Confirm that reversed presentation preserves the canonical reversed result.
- Confirm that no mapping claims the fictional divination system is historical.

## Pack-specific cautions

- **Brennos:** distinguish Helvetii/Central European La Tène material from later Insular Celtic motifs. Do not assign ancient meanings where archaeology preserves only form.
- **Yejide:** do not present the seed casting as Ifá. Treat Èṣù, Ọ̀ṣé Ṣàngó, Edan Ogboni, orí and other living sacred elements with appropriate review.
- **Ngaru:** Māori motifs and taonga require Māori review. Avoid tā moko and iwi-specific material unless explicitly approved.
- **Ame:** distinguish Shinto ritual elements from invented leaf divination. Imperial regalia references may require removal or careful restriction.
- **Amaru:** use documented khipu construction without claiming recovered spiritual knot meanings. Review Quechua spelling and Inka religious references.
- **Nahid:** smoke divination is fictional. Verify pre-Islamic dating and living Zoroastrian ritual restrictions.
- **Mictli:** verify Nahuatl spelling, calendrical combinations and glyph treatment with a Mexica/Nahua specialist.
